01 / AGENTIC RAG · VOICE + CHAT

Personal AI Voice & Chat Assistant

An AI version of me that recruiters can call or chat with, and that books the interview itself.
source ↗

Recruiters ask about my background by phone or chat. The assistant answers only from a retrieval index of my resume and GitHub READMEs, cites where each answer came from, and then puts a Google Meet on my calendar.

  • Two channels, one brain: a Vapi phone agent and a Streamlit web chat share the same knowledge base.
  • Grounded answers: top-k cosine retrieval over Gemini embeddings, with source citations on every chat reply.
  • Guardrails: refuses out-of-scope questions and prompt-injection attempts instead of making things up.
  • Real action: books interviews in Google Calendar with Meet links, Mon–Fri 9–5 IST.
  • Cheap to run: about $0.002 per chat session; the knowledge base is snapshotted offline, so no GitHub rate limits at runtime.

02 / COMPUTER VISION · VLMs · CAPSTONE

INDRA: Indian Dashcam Road Analytics

Reads Indian dashcam footage and reports plates, helmet violations, vehicle counts and scene conditions.
RoadReaper ↗

Off-the-shelf detectors struggle on Indian roads: mixed traffic, non-standard plates, autos, triple riding. INDRA combines YOLO detection, tracking and a vision-language model in one pipeline that processes video in batches and writes a single master report.

  • RoadReaper: my custom YOLO number-plate detector, trained on 38K Indian plate images, with about 95% plate accuracy.
  • YOLOv8m + ByteTrack to detect and track vehicles; Qwen3-VL-4B reads plates with multi-read consensus and describes the scene (weather, congestion, accidents).
  • 5 new violation classes added, such as triple riding, phone use while riding, wrong-lane riding and missing plates.
  • Plate validation that rejects false reads such as video watermarks and dashcam clock overlays, with Levenshtein clustering so each physical plate gives one row.
  • Production fixes: solved CUDA out-of-memory failures and added Prometheus monitoring.
  • Controlled experiments: RoadReaper + Qwen3-VL hybrid vs a Qwen-only pipeline across 53 dashcam videos, plus a VLM backbone comparison (Qwen3-VL-4B, Gemma, MiniCPM-V) with identical prompts and outputs.
  • Research: co-authored the IEEE conference paper “RoadReaper: A Custom YOLOv8 License Plate Detector for Multi-Modal Traffic Scene Understanding on Indian Roads.”

03 / VISION LLM · MOBILE

AI Bill Splitter

Snap a restaurant bill and everyone sees what they owe, including shared dishes, tax and tip.
source ↗

A Flutter app that sends a receipt photo to Gemini Vision, gets the line items back as structured JSON, and lets the group assign each dish. Nobody types prices by hand.

  • Vision → JSON: Gemini 2.5 Flash extracts items and prices; malformed or partial reads are validated.
  • Fair maths: shared items split across a subset of people; tax and tip spread by each person's subtotal, not divided equally.
  • Local bill history, dark and light themes, and export of the split as text or an image.
